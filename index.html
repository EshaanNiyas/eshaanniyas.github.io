About

A student engineer who ships.

Curiosity is the starting point; a working prototype is the proof.

I'm a student at Arcadia British School who spends most of his free time turning half-formed ideas into things that actually run — hardware on a breadboard, models in a notebook, pitches in front of a judging panel.

My work sits at the intersection of engineering, AI and sustainability. I like problems where the technical answer and the human one have to agree: a planter that keeps itself alive, a model that saves someone an hour, a product that a real person would pay for.

Outside of building, I compete — in STEM olympiads, entrepreneurial challenges and on the track for ADISSA — and I coach, volunteer and write about what I learn along the way.

01 Hardware · Sustainability

Hydrapot

A smart planter that keeps a plant alive without anyone remembering to water it. Soil-moisture sensing drives a self-regulating reservoir, so the plant draws what it needs and nothing is wasted — designed for people who want greenery without the guilt of a dead pot on the windowsill.
