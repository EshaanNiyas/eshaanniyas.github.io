About

This person builds things.

Eshaan Niyas is a student engineer and innovator exploring technology, AI, entrepreneurship, and solutions for real-world problems.

I'm a student at Arcadia British School in Dubai, and most of what I do starts the same way: something in the world seems fixable, so I try to fix it — in code, in hardware, or in a pitch.

That has taken me from the Pepsi Youth Hackathon and the Tortoise Tank innovation competition to the IITM Pravartak entrepreneurship program, the NYU Abu Dhabi Astronomy Camp, and a place in The Knowledge Society (TKS) Dubai 2026–2027.

I also write. Winning the Harvard Crimson Global Essay Competition for MENA & Africa, the Lumiere Scholars Essay Award 2025 and the HUSKO Asia Student Essay Competition 2026 taught me the same lesson the workshop does: an idea only counts once someone else can follow it.
