About

Curiosity drives everything I do.

As an Academic Scholar at Hartland International School, I’m passionate about exploring how science, technology, and leadership can create meaningful change. Whether I’m leading school initiatives, competing in STEM challenges, volunteering in my community, speaking at events, or representing my school in sport, I enjoy pushing myself beyond my comfort zone and embracing every opportunity to grow.

I believe that the best learning happens beyond the classroom. Every experience teaches resilience, empathy, and confidence - qualities that matter just as much as academic achievement.

My goal is simple: to keep learning, keep leading, and use my knowledge to make a positive impact on the lives of others.
